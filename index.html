What Factory Drops Does

We help shoppers discover clearance opportunities while keeping seller responsibility visible.

We review the listing

We may request seller identity, inventory information, product images, clearance context or supporting materials before publishing a Drop.

We explain what we know

We present the inventory story, seller information and material disclosures available to us. We do not claim more than the information supports.

You buy from the seller

When you choose to continue, you leave Factory Drops. The seller handles payment, shipping, returns, refunds and customer support.

IMPORTANT: REVIEW DOES NOT MEAN GUARANTEE.

Factory Drops may review seller and inventory information, but that review is not a certification, endorsement, warranty or guarantee. We do not independently test every item, inspect every unit, control a seller's conduct, or guarantee product quality, availability, authenticity, delivery, refund performance or after-sales service. Read the relevant Drop page and the seller's own terms before purchasing.
