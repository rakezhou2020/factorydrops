PRIVACY POLICY

How Factory Drops handles basic website, inquiry and referral information.

Starter policy notice.This policy is written for the current platform model. If you later add accounts, payments, advertising profiles, analytics vendors, newsletters or seller dashboards, update this policy before using those features.

1. Information we may collect

We may collect information you submit directly, such as your name, business name, email address, seller application information, messages and other information you choose to provide.

We may also collect basic technical and usage information such as browser type, device type, pages viewed, referring page, approximate location derived from IP address, and outbound link interactions when analytics tools are enabled.

2. How we use information

  • Operate and improve Factory Drops.
  • Review seller or inventory submissions.
  • Respond to inquiries.
  • Measure which Drops and pages receive interest.
  • Prevent abuse, fraud or security issues.
  • Comply with legal obligations.

3. Third-party seller websites

Factory Drops links to independent seller websites. Once you leave Factory Drops, the seller's privacy practices apply. We do not control how a third-party seller collects or uses your information.

4. Cookies and analytics

We may use necessary cookies and analytics technologies. If advertising, retargeting or additional tracking is added later, this section should be updated and consent controls may be required depending on the visitor's location.

5. Data sharing

We may share information with service providers that help operate the website, when required by law, or when needed to protect Factory Drops, users or others. We do not sell personal information merely because a visitor clicks a Drop.

6. Data retention and security

We keep information only as reasonably necessary for the purposes described above and use reasonable safeguards. No online system can promise absolute security.

7. Your choices

You may contact us to request access, correction or deletion of information you submitted, subject to applicable law and legitimate retention requirements.

8. Contact

Use the Contact page for privacy requests. A dedicated privacy email can be added before launch.
