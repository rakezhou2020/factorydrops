Before you leave

Is Factory Drops the seller?

No. Factory Drops is a product-discovery and clearance-information platform. Unless explicitly stated otherwise, the third-party seller handles the transaction.

Does Factory Drops verify the inventory?

We may request and review seller identity, inventory information, product images or other supporting material. This screening helps us decide whether to publish a Drop, but it is not independent inspection, certification or a guarantee.

Who is responsible for product quality?

The seller is responsible for the goods it sells and the promises it makes. Factory Drops does not provide a separate quality warranty or product guarantee for third-party offers.

Who handles shipping, returns or refunds?

The seller you purchase from handles fulfillment, shipping, returns, refunds and after-sales support.
