HOW IT WORKS

FROM LEFTOVER INVENTORY TO A FACTORY DROP.

Factory Drops is designed to make clearance inventory easier to understand before a shopper leaves for the seller's website.

A seller submits inventory

The seller provides product information, inventory context, images, quantities and a destination where the item is actually sold.

We may review supporting information

Depending on the Drop, we may ask for company details, inventory evidence, authorization or clarification about the clearance reason.

We publish a Drop page

The page explains the product and what we know about the inventory. It also identifies that the purchase will occur with a third-party seller.

The shopper chooses whether to continue

The outbound button sends the shopper to the seller's own website or sales page.

The seller handles the transaction

The seller controls price, payment, inventory, fulfillment, shipping, returns, refunds and after-sales support.

We can remove or revise listings

If information appears inaccurate, rights are unclear, complaints arise, or a listing no longer fits our standards, Factory Drops may edit, suspend or remove the Drop.

OUR REVIEW IS A PLATFORM SCREENING PROCESS.

It is not a product inspection service, certification program, escrow service, authenticity guarantee, quality guarantee, fulfillment guarantee or endorsement of every statement made by a seller.
