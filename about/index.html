ABOUT FACTORY DROPS

GOOD INVENTORY SHOULD NOT SIT FORGOTTEN IN A WAREHOUSE.

Factory Drops is a discovery platform for factory overstock, extra production, broken size runs, cancelled orders, seasonal inventory and other clearance opportunities.

WHY WE EXIST

Clearance is not one thing.

Useful inventory can become difficult to sell for ordinary commercial reasons: an order changes, a factory produces extra units, a size run becomes incomplete, packaging changes, or a season ends. Our job is to help surface those opportunities clearly.

WHAT WE ARE

Discovery first. Transaction second.

Factory Drops provides information, editorial context and outbound links. Unless explicitly stated otherwise, we are not the merchant of record and do not take payment for third-party seller offers.

HOW WE REVIEW

We can ask questions. We cannot control the seller.

We may review seller identity, inventory details, images, clearance reasons, authorization or other supporting information. This helps us decide whether a listing belongs on Factory Drops. It does not turn our review into a warranty or guarantee.

OUR LIMIT

We do not promise what we cannot verify.

We do not independently test every product or inspect every shipped unit. We cannot guarantee seller conduct, product quality, availability, authenticity, shipping performance, refund handling or customer service.
