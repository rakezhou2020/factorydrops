ABOUT FACTORY DROPS

良い商品を、倉庫に眠らせない。

Factory Drops は、工場の余剰在庫、追加生産分、サイズ欠け、キャンセル品、その他のクリアランス在庫と購入者をつなぎます。

Why Factory Drops exists

Overproduction, broken size runs, cancelled orders, seasonal changes and extra production happen every day. Factory Drops is designed to help useful inventory reach shoppers instead of sitting forgotten in storage.

What we are

We are a discovery and referral platform. We introduce products and sellers. The seller handles the actual transaction and after-sales service.
