FACTORY DROPS — DEMO SHOP

Copy the entire /demo-shop/ folder into the root of your Factory Drops website.

URLs after deployment:
/demo-shop/
/demo-shop/products/sienna.html
/demo-shop/products/luna.html
/demo-shop/products/aria.html
/demo-shop/cart.html
/demo-shop/checkout.html
/demo-shop/order-review.html

Homepage integration:
Open homepage-demo-shop-link.html and copy either link into your existing Factory Drops homepage.

Important:
- The cart and order data use browser localStorage only.
- No backend is required.
- No real payment is processed.
- The payment handoff page is intentionally designed as the future integration point for your payment API.
- Product 1 uses the supplied Sienna imagery/content direction.
- Products 2 and 3 are demo products with external stock-photo links; replace them later with your own products/images.
